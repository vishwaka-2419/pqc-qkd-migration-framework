Key-pool dynamics and the reserve-time signal under link-specific QKD
refill outages during the 24-hour reference run. (a)~Level of the paired-KME
key pool (capacity $B_{\max} = 5.12$~Mbit) of three overlay routes versus
time of day: dci-0 (solid), core-0 (dashed) and the unaffected dci-1
(dotted); every pool starts at 60\% of $B_{\max}$ and is full from 00:10,
and the three traces coincide at $B_{\max}$ outside the outages, so that only
one line is visible at 5.12~Mbit there. The shaded windows are the refill
outages injected into the scenario, 02:00--05:00 for core-0 and 09:00--15:00
for dci-0; during an outage the pool is drained only by the key demand of its
services (341~bit/s on the DCI routes, 102~bit/s on core-0). The thin grey
dotted horizontal line is the protected reserve $\beta B_{\max} = 0.256$~Mbit
($\beta = 0.05$), which Eq.~\eqref{eq:reserve} treats as unusable.
(b)~Predicted reserve time of the same three pools from
Eq.~\eqref{eq:reserve}, i.e.\ the time for which the usable pool would
sustain the planned key demand of the route, with the gold-SLA demotion
margin $T_{\min} = 2$~h (grey dashed horizontal line) and the re-entry
threshold $\kappa T_{\min} = 3$~h ($\kappa = 1.5$, grey dotted horizontal
line); both reference lines are labelled in the right margin. The dci-0
signal falls below the re-entry threshold at 10:00 and reaches the demotion
margin at 11:00, when the policy proactively demotes the 188 sessions of its
three gold services to non-QKD modes (OTN to PQC only, IPsec and MACsec to
PQ/T hybrid) at a pool level of 2.701~Mbit (52.8\% of $B_{\max}$); the
demoted services draw no further keys, so the level and the signal stay flat
(2.701~Mbit, 1.99~h) until the refill resumes at 15:00, after which the
services re-enter their QKD modes within five minutes, once the signal
exceeds 3~h. The shorter core-0 outage leaves its reserve above 10~h, far
from the margin, and triggers no demotion; dci-1 holds its full-pool reserve
of 3.96~h from 00:10 onwards. Both panels share the time axis. Planning-model
outputs of the seeded synthetic scenario (topology seed~7, simulation
seed~11); no carrier data are used.
