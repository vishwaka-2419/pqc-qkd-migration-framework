Reference architecture of the crypto-agile migration framework as implemented
by the simulator: four tiers inside the implementation boundary (grey dashed
rounded rectangle) and, below it, the encrypted carrier services that they
protect. From top to bottom: the service inventory, which supplies the
per-service context; the crypto-agile policy engine (tinted) with its
Mosca-margin prioritisation, mode selection along the ladder Traditional
$\rightarrow$ PQC $\rightarrow$ PQ/T hybrid $\rightarrow$ QKD-assisted
$\rightarrow$ PQ/T\,+\,QKD, reserve-time forecast with re-entry hysteresis,
fail-closed downgrade rules and selective overlay planner; the key sources
(PQ/T hybrid key establishment and the ETSI-014-inspired paired-KME pool
refilled by the QKD links); and the protocol adapters (IKEv2, MACsec/MKA,
OTN). Solid arrows carry planning and control information and key material;
the dashed path returns telemetry to the reserve-time forecast.
Section~\ref{sec:architecture} describes the tiers. Everything inside the
boundary is exercised by the seeded synthetic scenario (35 links,
60 services); no carrier data are used.
