Illustrative five-year cost of the migration with physical-hop accounting
(net present value, NPV, at the reference discount rate of 4\%).
(a)~Itemised five-year NPV of the twelve cost items of
Table~\ref{tab:costsplit}, one bar per item with its value in kEUR: the
upper group (light bars) is shared by both strategies and sums to
1\,100.6~kEUR, which is the cost of the PQC migration baseline; the lower
group (dark bars) is incremental to the selective QKD overlay and sums to
2\,838.6~kEUR, the assurance premium. Plain bars are capital expenditure,
hatched bars recurring operations (shared items over years 1--5, overlay
items over years 2--5, the overlay being commissioned in year~2); QKD endpoint pairs are
charged per physical hop (13 hops), trusted-site construction and operations
per trusted site (7 sites), and QKD service integration and optical
engineering per overlaid logical link (6 links).
(b)~Five-year NPV cost of the two strategies versus the QKD endpoint-pair
capex per physical hop (20--140~kEUR): the PQC migration baseline (dashed) is
independent of it, the PQC + selective QKD strategy (solid) rises by 12.5~kEUR
of NPV per kEUR of pair capex (13 pairs, bought in year~2 and discounted
one year at 4\%); the shaded
gap between the curves is the assurance premium, and the dotted vertical line
marks the reference capex of 100~kEUR (premium 2\,838.6~kEUR).
(c)~Assurance premium versus discount rate (0--10\%) for planning horizons
of 5 years (solid, circles), 7 years (dashed, squares) and 10 years (dotted,
triangles): 2\,994--2\,635~kEUR, 3\,366--2\,856~kEUR and 3\,924--3\,117~kEUR
respectively, so no combination in this range brings the premium close to
zero (Table~\ref{tab:discount}). The shared group (the policy
platform and its operations, PQC integration and operations, and the
phase 1--2 programme) is charged to both strategies and is therefore not
part of the premium. All
cost constants are illustrative planning assumptions, not carrier prices;
the overlay footprint is the planning-model output of the seeded synthetic
scenario (topology seed~7); no carrier data are used.
