Joint sensitivity of the selective QKD overlay to the designed hop limit
(rows: 50--100~km in 10~km steps and 120~km) and to the trusted-node policy
limit (columns: 0--6 and 8, the largest number of intermediate trusted nodes
a route may use). (a)~Physical QKD hops of the qualified overlay; the
bracketed number in each cell is the number of overlaid logical links.
(b)~Five-year assurance premium of the same overlay (NPV, MEUR). Cells are
shaded by the hop count in (a) and by the premium in (b) (one hue,
light = small, dark = large; colour bars), white separators mark the cell
boundaries, and the cell outlined in black is the reference configuration
(80~km, limit~4: 13 physical hops over 6 links, 2.8~MEUR premium). A route is
planned with the smallest number of equal hops not longer than the hop limit,
with one trusted node at every junction, and qualifies only if that number of
trusted nodes is within the policy limit (a core route is assumed to obtain
every approved trusted site it needs, except core-2 and core-5, which remain
one site short at every hop limit, and core-4, which has no qualified optical
path, so at most seven links, the four direct spans and the core routes
core-0, core-1 and core-3, ever qualify; the per-hop rate floor $R_{\min}$
never binds for hops of up to 120~km). Cells in which the hop count equals
the link count therefore contain direct spans only (3--4 hops, 3--4 links,
0.6--0.8~MEUR): no trusted-node route qualifies with a policy limit of 0 or 1,
and at a 50~km hop limit none qualifies below a limit of~6. Relaxing either
constraint admits the long-haul core routes step by step, e.g.\ along the
reference row (80~km) a policy limit of 3, 4 and 5 gives 8, 13 and 19 hops
(1.7, 2.8 and 4.2~MEUR); the full seven-link overlay needs 24 hops at a 60~km
hop limit but only 14 hops at 120~km (5.4 versus 3.0~MEUR), and the largest
footprint, 26 hops over 6 links (5.9~MEUR), arises at 50~km with a limit
of~8. All values are planning-model outputs of the seeded synthetic scenario
(topology seed~7, simulation seed~11); no carrier data are used.
