Spread of the headline results of the proposed policy over 20 synthetic
topologies, with the reference topology highlighted: the scenario generator
was run with topology seeds 1--20 (seed~7 is the reference topology used
throughout the manuscript) and every topology was planned and simulated once
with the reference parameters and simulation seed~11 (one 24-hour run per
seed). Each panel shows one metric of these 20 runs.
(a) Overlaid logical links, i.e.\ links that receive the selective QKD
overlay: 4--10 links (mean 7.0, standard deviation 1.5, median 7, quartiles 6
and 8; reference topology 6).
(b) Physical QKD hops of the overlay: 6--19 hops (mean 13.45, s.d.\ 3.8,
median 13.5, quartiles 10.75 and 16.25; reference 13).
(c) QKD-backed share of the QKD-preferred rekeys during the run:
91.8--95.2\% (mean 94.3\%, s.d.\ 0.8 percentage points, median 94.4\%,
quartiles 94.2 and 94.8\%; reference 94.2\%); the lowest share belongs to
seed~15, the only topology whose overlay consists of direct spans only
(6 links over 6 hops), which also has the fewest hops and the smallest premium
of the set.
(d) Five-year assurance premium, the NPV of the QKD overlay above the PQC
migration baseline: 1170--4163\,kEUR (mean 2893\,kEUR, s.d.\ 854\,kEUR,
median 2958\,kEUR, quartiles 2249 and 3481\,kEUR; reference 2839\,kEUR).
Each dot is one topology seed, spread horizontally only so that no dot hides
another (the horizontal position carries no information); the open diamond at
the centre of each panel is the reference seed~7, which lies inside or at the
lower edge of the interquartile range in every panel. Boxes span the
interquartile range (lower to upper quartile), the heavy line inside a box is
the median, and the whiskers extend to the minimum and the maximum, so no
seed lies outside the whiskers. All values are planning-model outputs of
seeded synthetic scenarios (topology seeds 1--20, simulation seed~11); no
carrier data are used.
